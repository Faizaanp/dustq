## G1 verdict: PASS at P0 (gradient level)

fp32-vs-bf16 Dust gradients on M/1M/K256, one batch, mean cosine **+0.9910** over every
parameter group; per-group minimum +0.938 (c_k, the expected float-sensitive line), lm_head
1.0000. bf16 is usable for the fp path on the L4. The strict noise calibration (seed-vs-seed
cosine on the same configuration) is schedule-able as a one-line addition to the probe
session; at this level (worst group 0.938, mean 0.991) the conclusion is robust to that
calibration.

inside the 3-seed bf16 spread (+0.0017 ≈ 0.25σ), and at P3 the fp32/bf16 test losses differ by

criteria; bf16 is usable for the fp path on the L4, and Tier B does not need an fp32 arm.
