## The K=1024 exception (and its twin at K=4096)

- **K=4096 s1 = 6.0135**: the history caught the event live — a step-12 update collapsed the
  model to ~uniform prediction (val 8.333 ≈ ln 4096 = 8.318) from train 7.183 (pre-update);
  full recovery over ~14 steps; final ≈ best (Δ 0.004), so the paper's own >0.25 rejection
  rule does not apply. The cell still passed (mean Δ 0.023).
- **K=1024 s1 = 6.2144**: converged +0.280 with final ≈ best — consistent with the same
  spike-recovery signature (curve still unexamined); its siblings sit at Δ 0.009.
- Mechanism and citations (WATCH-2): matches GradientStabilizer (arXiv:2502.17055 — rare
  gradient-norm spike → oversized update → transient corruption → recovery), PolyPythias
  outlier-seed spikes (arXiv:2503.09543), MEAZO grouped-ZO-SGD seed divergence
  (arXiv:2605.03869), and the EMNLP-2025 cross-seed divergence study. At a 61-step budget one
  event spends ~23% of training recovering.
- Treatment: seeds are reported as-is; no seed is replaced on its outcome; the paper
  reports mean ± sd per cell (the emerging community standard on this exact model/data).
  Optional owner decision: 2-3 extra K=1024 seeds (~4-6 L4-h) to characterize the variance
  distribution for the reproduction section.
