## Optimization leads

(Leads only — nothing adopted without owner approval. Bottlenecks: 2.48–3.18× per-forward re-quantization overhead; ~2.5–2.8× minimal-loop slowdown; and the K=1024 single-seed +0.28-nat divergence.)

1. **NEW — estimator seed variance/stability (for the K=1024 divergence):**
   - **MEAZO** — Dbouk, Gazagnadou, Reisser, Louizos, "On Adaptivity in Zeroth-Order Optimization", arXiv:2605.03869 (May 5, 2026) [verified: abs page + full text, 2026-10-19/20]: reports that grouped ZO-SGD "can be brittle, particularly toward the end of training, where **some random seeds numerically diverge**" (Fig. 6; App. E.5/Fig. 15), and that adaptive ZO (ZO-Adam, MEAZO) widens the stable step-size window vs ZO-SGD. The most direct citable precedent that a ZO estimator can have divergent seeds under identical config. Suggests checking our divergence for (i) late-training phase, (ii) optimizer adaptivity, (iii) grouped/block update structure — before attributing it to the quantized forward.
   - **"Convergence and Divergence of Language Models under Different Random Seeds"** (EMNLP 2025 main, ACL Anthology 2025.emnlp-main.1675) [verified via Anthology]: four-phase cross-seed convergence/divergence pattern in LM training — canonical citation that seed-level divergence is a known LM-training phenomenon independent of the estimator.
   - **PolyPythias** — "Stability and Outliers across Fifty Language Model Pre-Training Runs", arXiv:2503.09543 [verified via full text]: 45 Pythia runs (9 seeds × 5 sizes); outlier seeds with loss spikes (410M seeds 3 & 4); training-map "forks" as early instability signals — a framework for classifying our K=1024 seed as an outlier run.
   - **GradientStabilizer** — arXiv:2502.17055 (v2 Aug 3, 2026; ICML 2026) [verified abstract]: rare gradient-norm spikes → oversized updates/corrupted optimizer state → divergence; drop-in magnitude stabilizer; evaluated in FP16 LLM pretraining and **FP4 quantization-aware pretraining**. Candidate mitigation if our divergence traces to update spikes.
   - **The Blessing of Dimensionality** — arXiv:2602.00170 (Qiu/Miikkulainen group) [verified abstract]: rise-peak-degrade dynamics under fixed hyperparameters in ES/GRPO — documented fixed-config fragility of population-based estimators. **SubZero+** — arXiv:2608.15665 [verified abstract]: ZO noise amplification in Adam moment buffers; multi-query differences + periodic restarts as mitigation.
   - **Community evidence on our exact model/data**: Noise PR #10 paired seeds — effect swings 1.3982% (seed 1) vs 0.0798% (seed 2), author declines significance [verified 2026-10-18]; JaydenTeoh's merged log: same config, seed 0 → 3.9830 vs 3.9730 across scripts/hardware [verified 2026-10-18]; erodola's PR #20 reports **mean ± sd over 3–5 seeds** and compares "within seed noise" [verified 2026-10-20]. Multi-seed mean ± sd is becoming the reporting standard for exactly our setup — our paper should match it for any seed-sensitive claim.
2. **torch.compile / graph breaks (new datapoints, both from Noise):** PR #9 "Compile probe construction" cut Track 2 from 22 to 21 min [verified 2026-10-18]; PR #10 hit a real Inductor sparse-mask construction failure with the attention window mask during warmup, fixed by hoisting mask construction out of the compiled `block_forward` [verified 2026-10-18].
